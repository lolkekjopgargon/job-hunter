His Game Design is **NOT** limited to numbers, metrics, or spreadsheets. He handles the full creative and mechanical design cycle:

### Balance & Simulation Workflow
Pavel does **NOT** manually tweak large spreadsheet cells one-by-one. Instead, he automates balance validation:
1. **Design & formalization:** Personally designs the mechanic, system logic, and rules;
2. **AI-assisted scripting:** Writes verification scripts in collaboration with AI tools;
3. **Automated simulation:** Runs extensive automated simulations;

6. **AI-assisted analysis:** Analyzes the simulation output with AI assistance;
7. **Flaw detection:** Looks for dominant strategies ("имба"), edge cases, unwanted loops, probability skews, and balance breaks;
8. **System tuning:** Adjusts the rules, data architecture, or parameters based on simulation findings.

- Pavel is **NOT** a Data Scientist, professional programmer, statistician, or quantitative analyst.
- This is his workflow as a **Game Designer** who leverages AI and automation to skip manual spreadsheet grind.
